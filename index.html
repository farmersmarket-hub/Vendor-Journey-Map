  • RetailerResells packaged goods
  • Hot-food VendorPrepares ready-to-eat foods

  • Submit Market Vendor Request Form ↗
  • Wait 3–5 days for approval email
  • Receive your Vendor PIN & Vendor ID
i
Keep your PIN secure and memorable - you'll need it every market day to log transactions!

Complete within 14 days or participation may be paused

Customer Registration

First Time Phase 1
A Register at Market
  • Visit market check-in table

C Return to Market

Keep your phone charged for easy QR access!

Market Day Preparation: What to Do

Weekly Module: D, E, F, G
D Complete Upcoming Market Form
  • Submit Upcoming Market Form ↗ (sent weekly)
  • Confirm attendance & list products
  • Request any equipment needed
E What This Form Does
  • Confirms your attendance
  • Preloads products into Transaction Form
  • Informs logistics (space, power, equipment)
Check email/texts weekly for PGL updates
F & G Prepare for Market Day
  • Farmers: finalize products by Wednesday night
  • Charge all devices; bring backup battery
  • Prepare inventory and signage
  • Due before each market (sent weekly)
Critical: Without the Upcoming Market Form, you cannot participate!

Prepare for Next Week

Vendor Market Day Operations

3 parts Operations Module: I–N
I Check-in at Manager Table
  • Arrive & set up your table
  • Check Wi-Fi/data connection
  • Open Transaction Form → enter Vendor PIN
  • Verify products appear in pick-lists
  • Check in at PGL Table
J During Market (Log Every Sale!)
  • Use Transaction Form ↗ for each sale
  • Prefer scanning attendee's QR code
  • Or enter Neighbor ID manually
Neighbor ID format: REACH-12345
QR scanning is faster!
K & L End of Market & Check-Out
  • Complete End of Market Vendor Invoice Form ↗
  • Wait for "submitted successfully" message
  • Visit PGL Check-out Table (required!)
🚨 You MUST check out at PGL table before leaving!
